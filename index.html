Our Story

Rooted in the Mountains,
Served on the Streets

Himalayan Street Co. was born from a deep love for the bold, honest flavours of North-East India and the high-altitude kitchens of Nepal and Bhutan. Every dish we serve carries a piece of that mountain spirit — slow-cooked, hand-crafted, and full of heart.

From our hand-folded momos and fiery chutneys to our hearty thukpa broths and grain bowls bursting with nutrition, we bring the best of the Himalayas right to your plate.

Steamed Momos

Starting at ₹129

Hand-folded to perfection, our momos are made fresh every morning with a filling of your choice — spiced vegetables, chicken, or paneer. Steamed until soft and served with our fiery house-made tomato-sesame chutney.
